# 2026-08-04 tester release

This is an unofficial LineageOS 23 tester build for Nothing Phone (3)
(`metroid`). It is not an official LineageOS release or a daily-driver claim.

## Download

Host the complete OTA ZIP on PixelDrain, AndroidFileHost, SourceForge, or another
service that supports files larger than 4 GiB. Users must download and sideload
one unchanged ZIP; do not split it for recovery.

## Verified

- Recovery sideload and target-slot boot.
- Enforcing SELinux and encrypted userdata.
- Payload equals target-files for every OTA partition; AVB graph verifies.
- Fingerprint enrollment artwork and lockscreen indication placement.
- Rear/front photo and video, front screen flash, logical SAT 0.6x/1x/3x,
  seamless zoom while recording, and FHD60 in controlled tests.
- Stock Nothing vibrator service, RC, RichTap/AW dependencies, and SFDC load.

## Known issues

- Face unlock enrollment is not working.
- Camera zoom/lens behavior still needs wider testing across applications and
  modes. UHD/4K routing needs community validation.
- Haptics remain noticeably weaker than Nothing OS despite the stock stack.
- Calls, SMS, mobile data, IMS, emergency calling, and multi-SIM require physical
  SIM testing.
- Unlocked bootloader means no Widevine L1, strong Play Integrity, or trusted
  HDCP state.
